# Azentio Leave and Attendance Policy (v3.2)
**Document ID**: POL-HR-LEAVE-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Azentio Enterprise Employees (India, UAE, Singapore)

## Section 1: Annual Leave Entitlements
All permanent full-time employees are entitled to:
- **Earned Leave (EL)**: 18 working days per calendar year, credited pro-rata at 1.5 days per completed month.
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 12 days per calendar year (cannot be combined with Earned Leave).
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 10 days per calendar year. Medical certificate mandatory if exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## Section 2: Probation Period Leave Guidelines
2.1 During the standard 6-month probation period, employees are eligible to avail Casual Leave on a pro-rata basis of **1 day per completed month of service**.  
2.2 Earned Leave cannot be availed during probation except under exceptional medical emergencies with approval from the Head of HR.  
2.3 Unapproved absence during probation extends the probation period by the equivalent number of absent days.

## Section 3: Public & Regional Holidays
3.1 Bengaluru & Pune offices observe 10 mandatory festival holidays plus 2 optional floating holidays per calendar year.  
3.2 The official holiday calendar is published on the Azentio Intranet portal every December.

## Section 4: Working Hours & Hybrid Work
4.1 Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday, with a 60-minute lunch break.  
4.2 Technology and Engineering roles are eligible for 2 days Work-From-Home (WFH) per week with prior manager approval.
