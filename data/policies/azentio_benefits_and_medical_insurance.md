# Azentio Employee Benefits & Medical Insurance Policy (v2.0)
**Document ID**: POL-BEN-MED-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Full-Time Permanent Employees

## Section 1: Group Medical Cover (GMC)
1.1 Every employee is enrolled from Day 1 into the Group Medical Cover policy underwritten by Star Health / ICICI Lombard.  
1.2 **Base Sum Insured**: INR 5,00,000 (Five Lakhs) floater policy covering Employee, Spouse, and up to 2 dependent children.  
1.3 Parents / Parents-in-law cover can be opted under a subsidized co-pay model during the annual enrollment window.  
1.4 Cashless hospitalization is supported across 10,000+ network hospitals nationwide.

## Section 2: Outpatient (OPD) & Wellness Benefits
2.1 Annual executive health check-up is 100% reimbursed for employees aged 30 and above.  
2.2 Teleconsultation via Practo / MediBuddy is free and unlimited for all employees and immediate family members.  
2.3 Annual wellness allowance of up to INR 12,000 is available for gym memberships, mental health counseling, or fitness trackers.

## Section 3: Provident Fund (PF) & Gratuity
3.1 Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) deduction is 12% of basic salary matched equally by Azentio.  
3.2 Gratuity is payable per the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 after completion of 5 continuous years of service.
