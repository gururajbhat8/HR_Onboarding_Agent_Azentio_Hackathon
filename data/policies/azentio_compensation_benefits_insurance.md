# Azentio Total Rewards, Compensation & Health Benefits Policy (v4.2)
**Document ID**: POL-HR-BENEFITS-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Full-Time Permanent Employees (India, UAE, Singapore)

## Section 1: Group Medical & Hospitalization Insurance
1.1 All full-time employees and their immediate dependants (spouse and up to two children) are covered under the Azentio Group Health Insurance scheme with a base coverage of **INR 5,00,000 per family unit**.  
1.2 Dependent parents may be enrolled under a subsidized top-up policy with coverage options of INR 3,00,000 or INR 5,00,000 during the first 30 days from joining.  
1.3 Pre-existing diseases are covered from Day 1 with cashless claim settlement across 6,500+ network hospitals via the MediAssist TPA portal.

## Section 2: Outpatient (OPD) & Annual Executive Health Checkup
2.1 Employees in Senior Software Engineer grades and above are eligible for outpatient consultation reimbursements up to **INR 15,000 per financial year**.  
2.2 An annual comprehensive health checkup package is fully sponsored by Azentio at partnered diagnostic centers (Apollo, Manipal, Fortis) every October.

## Section 3: Statutory Retirement Benefits (PF & Gratuity)
3.1 Provident Fund (PF) contributions are matched at the statutory rate of 12% of basic salary in accordance with the Employees' Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act.  
3.2 Gratuity is payable upon continuous service of 5 or more years in Azentio, calculated at 15 days of basic salary for each completed year of service.

## Section 4: Remote Work & Wellness Allowance
4.1 Engineering and Operations staff receive a one-time Home Office Setup subsidy of **INR 20,000** for ergonomic chair, monitors, and desk equipment upon completing document verification.  
4.2 A monthly broadband reimbursement of up to **INR 1,500** is credited with monthly payroll upon submission of ISP bills in the ZingHR portal.
