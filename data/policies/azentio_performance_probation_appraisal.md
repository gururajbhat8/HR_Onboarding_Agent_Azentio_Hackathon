# Azentio Probation, Performance Evaluation & Career Growth Policy (v2.8)
**Document ID**: POL-HR-PERF-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Newly Hired and Existing Staff

## Section 1: Probation Period & 30-60-90 Day Milestones
1.1 All new lateral and campus hires undergo a standard probation period of **six (6) calendar months** from their joining date.  
1.2 Reporting managers must conduct formal check-ins at Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90 to assess technical alignment, sprint velocity, and corporate culture integration.  
1.3 Confirmation is formalized at month 6 following an OKR review score of >= 3.5 out of 5.0 and zero pending compliance deliverables.

## Section 2: Probation Extension & Separation Guidelines
2.1 In the event of performance shortfall or extended medical leave (> 15 days), the probation period may be extended once for a maximum duration of three (3) months.  
2.2 During probation, notice period for separation from either employee or employer is thirty (30) calendar days. Post confirmation, standard notice period is ninety (90) calendar days.

## Section 3: Annual Appraisal Cycle & Merit Increments
3.1 The Azentio Annual Performance Appraisal cycle runs from January 1 to December 31, with evaluations conducted in February and merit compensation revisions effective April 1.  
3.2 Employees who join on or before September 30 of the appraisal year are eligible for pro-rated merit increments and performance bonuses.
