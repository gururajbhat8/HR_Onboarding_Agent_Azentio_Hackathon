# Azentio Business Travel, Relocation & Expense Reimbursement Policy (v3.1)
**Document ID**: POL-FIN-TRAVEL-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Azentio Associates Traveling on Official Duty

## Section 1: Relocation Assistance & Settlement Allowance
1.1 Lateral new hires relocating from a different metropolitan city are eligible for relocation assistance of up to **INR 50,000** covering packers & movers and transit flights.  
1.2 Relocation reimbursement requires submission of original GST tax invoices within 45 days of joining and carries a twelve (12) month service retention agreement.  
1.3 Up to seven (7) nights of corporate transit accommodation is arranged at Azentio-partnered hotels near the base office.

## Section 2: Domestic & International Client Travel
2.1 Travel must be pre-approved by the Practice Head via the Concur travel portal at least 7 days in advance.  
2.2 Domestic daily out-of-pocket allowance (per-diem) is **INR 1,500 per day** in Tier-1 cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR) and INR 1,200 in Tier-2 cities.  
2.3 International per-diem rates: UAE (AED 350/day), Singapore (SGD 120/day), UK (GBP 80/day).

## Section 3: Late Night Commute & Cab Facilities
3.1 Employees working on client releases or emergency deployments beyond 8:00 PM IST are entitled to an Azentio corporate Uber/Ola ride home reimbursed at 100%.  
3.2 Prior notification to the immediate delivery manager is required before scheduling night commute rides.
