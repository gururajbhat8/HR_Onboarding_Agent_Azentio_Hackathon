# Azentio Code of Conduct & Enterprise Compliance Policy (v3.5)
**Document ID**: POL-HR-ETHICS-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Azentio Enterprise Personnel Worldwide

## Section 1: Professional Integrity & Conflict of Interest
1.1 Employees must avoid any personal activity, investment, or association that compromises their independent judgment in Azentio business decisions.  
1.2 Secondary employment, moonlighting, or consulting for third-party entities is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from the Chief People Officer.  
1.3 Acceptance of gifts or hospitality valued over INR 2,500 from vendors, clients, or banking partners is prohibited and must be declared to the Compliance Desk.

## Section 2: Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) & Anti-Discrimination
2.1 Azentio maintains a zero-tolerance policy against any form of workplace harassment, discrimination, or retaliation based on gender, religion, race, disability, or sexual orientation.  
2.2 The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) investigates all formal complaints confidentially within a mandatory 30-day statutory window.  
2.3 Formal POSH grievances must be addressed to `icc.chairperson@azentio.com` or escalated to Lead HRBP `priya.sharma@azentio.com`.

## Section 3: Client Data Confidentiality & Banking Regulations
3.1 As an enterprise financial technology provider, Azentio personnel handle core banking systems, AML/KYC data, and institutional records subject to RBI, MAS, and DFSA banking privacy regulations.  
3.2 Employees must never store client banking data on personal storage drives, cloud repositories, or public pastebins.  
3.3 Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) signed upon joining remain legally binding for 24 months post-employment.

## Section 4: Whistleblower Protection & Compliance Escalation
4.1 Any employee witnessing fraudulent accounting, bribery, document forgery, or severe security violations is protected under the Azentio Whistleblower Framework.  
4.2 Reports may be submitted anonymously to `ethics.hotline@azentio.com`. Retaliation against any whistleblower results in immediate termination of employment.
