# Azentio Information Security & Device Acceptable Use Policy (v4.0)
**Document ID**: POL-SEC-DEVICE-2026  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Applicability**: All Engineering, Product, and Enterprise Operations Staff

## Section 1: Laptop & Workstation Asset Allocation
1.1 Standard corporate laptops (MacBook Pro for Engineering or Dell Latitude for Operations) are provisioned by IT Ops 3 business days prior to the official joining date.  
1.2 Asset dispatch is strictly contingent upon 100% completion and approval of Pre-Joining Identity & Address Verification.  
1.3 Employees must sign the Digital Asset Handover Agreement upon physical delivery of the equipment.

## Section 2: Account Security & Multi-Factor Authentication
2.1 All employees are issued an `@azentio.com` corporate Google Workspace and Microsoft Active Directory account.  
2.2 Hardware security keys (FIDO2) or Microsoft Authenticator MFA are mandatory for all logins.  
2.3 Passwords must be at least 14 characters in length and expire every 90 days.

## Section 3: Development Tools & Cloud Access
3.1 Access to GitHub Enterprise, AWS Staging/Prod environments, and Docker registries is restricted to Engineering and DevOps personnel.  
3.2 Access requests require dual sign-off from the Engineering Manager (Ananya Roy) and Compliance Lead (Deepak Mehta).  
3.3 Direct access to production databases requires an emergency Jira change request ticket with maximum 4-hour temporary lease.

## Section 4: Data Protection & Prohibited Software
4.1 Commercial generative AI tools (e.g. personal ChatGPT) must not be fed proprietary client banking data or customer PII.  
4.2 Torrent clients, unauthorized VPNs, and cryptocurrency mining tools are strictly prohibited and monitored via CrowdStrike EDR.
