DEMO ONBOARDING POLICY

All new employees must submit the identity and address documents listed in their onboarding checklist before their joining date. HR reviews submitted documents. If a document is unclear or incorrect, HR changes its status to Correction Requested and records the reason.

All employees must read and acknowledge the information security and acceptable use policies on or before their joining date. Engineering employees receive development-tool access only after the required approvals are complete.

For questions not covered by this policy, employees should contact the HR Operations team.
